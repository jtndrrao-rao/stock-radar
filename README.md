<h2 align="center">🔄 The Old Way vs This Way</h2>

<div align="center">

| 😩 The old way | ✅ With Stock Radar |
|:---|:---|
| 😩 Reorder lists built by gut feel | ✅ Order-up-to level from demand over lead time, a 30-day cycle and safety days |
| 😩 Dead stock nobody clears | ✅ Dead stock list with the cash that clearing at a discount would release |
| 😩 Overstock keeps getting reordered | ✅ Overstock list to pause buying until cover comes down |
| 😩 Purchase orders built manually | ✅ A purchase plan by supplier you can copy as a PO list |
| 😩 Opaque numbers | ✅ Click any row to see the reasoning behind it |

</div>

#### 📊 Overview KPIs
Stock value, days of inventory, stock-out risk, capital locked, healthy stock share and suggested purchase orders.

</td>

#### 🛒 Action Centre
Reorder alerts, dead stock and overstock in three tabs, with a purchase plan by supplier.

</td>

Which SKUs drive consumption, and how fast each one moves.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🔒 Capital Locked
Biggest capital traps by SKU, plus a clearance what-if that shows the cash released.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🔎 SKU Explorer
Every item in one sortable table with movement, cover and stock value.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">
